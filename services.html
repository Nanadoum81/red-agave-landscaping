Landscape Design and Installation

Your yard, reimagined as one coherent design instead of a patchwork of fixes. We plan the layout, planting, rock work, and water as a single system, then install it with our own crew.

Whether you are starting from bare dirt or rescuing a tired yard, you get a design that suits the house, the light, and the way you actually live outside.

What's included

  • On-site design walkthrough at your estimate visit
  • Desert-ready plant selection for your sun and soil
  • Rock, gravel, and boulder placement
  • Soil preparation and finish grading

Good for: bare-dirt new builds, full yard remodels, and tired yards that need one coherent plan instead of patchwork fixes.

Irrigation and Sprinklers

Water is the most expensive mistake in a Phoenix yard, too much or too little. Red Agave installs and repairs drip and sprinkler systems that put water where roots need it and nowhere else.

Every system is zoned for the plants it serves and set on a schedule matched to the season, so your landscape drinks deeply without runoff or waste.

What's included

  • New drip and sprinkler installation
  • Smart timers and seasonal scheduling
  • Leak checks, broken line and head repairs
  • Drip conversion for beds and desert planting

Good for: high water bills, dry spots, overspray and flooding, and older systems with broken heads or leaking lines.

Pavers and Hardscaping

Patios, walkways, and courtyards are the bones of an outdoor room. We build them on properly compacted bases with clean edges and drainage that respects monsoon season.

Hardscaping pairs with planting in one plan, so your patio, paths, and beds read as one finished space instead of three separate projects.

What's included

  • Paver patios, walkways, and courtyards
  • Seating walls, steps, and borders
  • Gravel and decorative rock ground cover
  • Base prep and drainage done right

Good for: patios, walkways, and courtyards that need to stay level and drain properly through monsoon season.

Desert and Xeriscape Conversions

Trade a thirsty lawn for a landscape that belongs here. Xeriscape conversions remove grass, reshape the beds, and plant sculptural desert species that look better every year while sipping water.

Done well, a conversion raises curb appeal and cuts the weekly work to almost nothing. Done poorly, it is just gravel. We do the first kind.

What's included

  • Grass removal and bed reshaping
  • Drought-smart plant palettes with year-round structure
  • Decorative rock, boulders, and steel edging
  • Drip irrigation fitted to the new planting

Good for: homeowners trading a thirsty lawn for lower water use, real curb appeal, and near-zero weekly upkeep.

Artificial Turf

Green in January, green in July, and never on the mower schedule. Modern turf suits play areas, dog runs, and side yards where real grass struggles in Phoenix heat.

We prep and drain the base properly, seam the turf cleanly, and finish the edges so it reads as a lawn, not a rug.

What's included

  • Turf for yards, play areas, and dog runs
  • Pet-friendly turf options
  • Compacted base and drainage preparation
  • Clean, finished edging and seams

Good for: play areas, dog runs, side yards, and any spot where real grass struggles in Phoenix heat.
