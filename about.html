Meet Jose, and the crew behind the name

Red Agave Landscaping is a locally owned Phoenix metro company. When you reach out, you are talking with the owner's team from the first reply to the final walkthrough.

Why Jose started Red Agave

Jose built Red Agave around a simple frustration: homeowners were paying for yards that looked fine for a season, then fell apart in the heat, drank water like lawns back east, or never got finished the way the proposal promised.

So Red Agave works differently. The owner scopes your project in person. The design respects the desert instead of fighting it. The proposal is written, the crew that starts your job finishes it, and the site is left clean enough to enjoy the same evening.

That is the whole formula: straight answers, desert-smart design, and work that holds up in August.
